**Freedrive** releases the arm's motor torque so you can move it by hand, and streams the arm's measured joint angles up to its [digital twin](/use-cyberwave/digital-twins). It is **read-only**: Cyberwave sends the arm no commands while Freedrive is active, so the twin follows the physical robot instead of the other way around.

---

## Assign it

Open the twin in the [dashboard](https://cyberwave.com/dashboard), run **Calibrate** if you haven't yet, then **Assign Controller** → **Freedrive**.

Like every other controller, only one runs at a time — assigning **Keyboard**, **Local Teleop**, or an AI policy replaces Freedrive. Freedrive goes through the same calibration check as the others.

<Warning>
  The arm goes limp the moment Freedrive starts. Support it by hand before
  assigning it, or it will fall under its own weight.
</Warning>

---

## When to use it

- **Check your calibration and mounting.** Move the arm through its range by hand and confirm the twin tracks it.
- **Find a pose by hand.** Position the real arm where you want it, then read the joint values off the twin.
- **Rehearse a motion** before recording it with a controller that can drive the arm.

For **recording training datasets**, use [Local Teleop](/use-cyberwave/environment-editor/teleoperation) instead — it captures leader-arm motion at a much higher rate. Freedrive publishes at a lower rate, which produces sparse trajectories.

---

## Availability

Freedrive is available on the [SO-101](https://cyberwave.com/the-robot-studio/so101). Support depends on the arm's driver being able to disable torque, so it is not offered for every arm in the catalog — check the twin's **Assign Controller** list.

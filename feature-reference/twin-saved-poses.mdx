## What it is

Poses are named joint-angle snapshots you can save and re-apply later, from
the Poses section of a twin's right-hand panel (below Manual Control /
Joint Editing).

## Where it works

- **Edit mode**: save/edit/delete/apply poses against the twin's rest state.
  Applying (**GO**) drives the joints there gradually.
- **Live mode**: save captures the twin's current live telemetry (not the
  rest state); applying a pose sends it to the real device over MQTT.
- **Simulate / Playground mode**: save captures the current simulated joint
  state; applying (**Snap**) **teleports** the simulated robot to the pose
  instantly instead of driving it there gradually. A running controller can
  override the snap on its next tick — pause it first.

## Home (edit pose)

The panel also lists the twin's authored edit/home pose as a **Home (edit
pose)** item (with a **Preview**). Triggering it returns the robot to that
pose — **Snap** in Edit (applied and saved) and in Simulate/Playground
(instant), **Drive** (rate-limited real move) in Live. See
[Joint home positions](/feature-reference/twin-joint-home-positions).

## Scopes

A pose can be saved at twin, environment, or asset scope, controlling
whether it's specific to this twin, this environment, or shared across every
instance of the asset. On a twin, the **Twin** scope lists only poses
authored on that twin (asset-shared poses appear under the **Asset** scope).
